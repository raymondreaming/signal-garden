A FEW WAYS IN

There is no wrong turn.

Move, then click. Your pointer bends the field. Click to leave up to six forces behind. Use Attract, Repel, or Swirl, then Clear to start over.

Play with time. Space pauses the animation. 1–4 load systems. R changes the seed. S saves a recipe. H toggles this guide. Escape stops evolution and clears overlays.

Take something with you. Export a PNG of the current moment, or share a recipe link. Saved recipes include the controls, but not temporary pointer forces or animation time.
